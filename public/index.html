How it works and what it knows

A YOLO detector finds every car, motorcycle, bus and truck, then a classifier (CLIP image features + logistic regression) names each car that is at least 48 pixels across. When it is sure it shows the model (for example ID:3 Proton Saga 0.88); when only the brand is clear it shows the brand; otherwise it just says car. Very small cars and motorcycles are boxed but not named.

The Show setting: Nearby cars only hides vehicles narrower than a tenth of the frame (distant cars are too small to name reliably); Named cars only hides every box that would just say car. Hidden cars are still detected, just not drawn.

Video: the page takes a frame every half second (you can change this), sends each frame to the same recogniser, follows each car from frame to frame, and gives it the name it was given most often. Press play to watch the boxes on the video.

Photos and frames you upload are processed and not stored. Example photos: Wikimedia Commons, see credits.
