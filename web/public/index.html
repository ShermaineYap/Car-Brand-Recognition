Car brand and model recognition

Upload a photo of a car on a Malaysian road. Each car is boxed and named, for example Perodua Myvi.

Or try:

How it works and what it knows

A YOLO detector finds each vehicle, then a classifier (CLIP image features + logistic regression) names cars covering at least 3% of the photo. Smaller background cars get a thin grey box. Low-confidence guesses are shown in grey with a question mark.

It knows 12 models; anything else should come out as Other car.

On 548 hand-checked Wikimedia Commons photos it names the right model about 87% of the time. It is weaker on pre-2016 generations and its main mix-ups are Myvi vs Axia, Vios vs City and X70 vs X50. Photos you upload are processed and not stored.
